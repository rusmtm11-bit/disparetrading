About us

Dispare Trading — auto parts importer and wholesale supplier

We bring goods from abroad, clear them through customs in our own name and supply businesses across Russia. Fewer links in the chain means clearer prices and paperwork.

01 / What we do

Import and wholesale distribution

Dispare Trading (DISPER LLC, ООО «ДИСПЭР») imports auto parts and tyres and supplies them wholesale across Russia. Our customers are repair shops, service chains, retailers, distributors and other companies that need steady supplies under a contract.

Goods clear customs in our company's name, are stored at our warehouse in the Moscow region and are checked before shipment. Inside Russia, there is nobody between customs and you but us — so we take responsibility for every item.

02 / How we operate
  1. Transparent paperworkContract, invoice, UPD and closing documents for every deal.
  2. Clean payments onlyBank transfer to the company account with VAT. No undocumented cash.
  3. Responsibility for qualityWarranty and defect handling are written into the contract.
  4. Long-term partnershipsWe focus on regular partners rather than one-off deals.
  5. E-documents and product markingWe exchange documents electronically with counterparties and use the national "Chestny Znak" marking system for goods that require it.
03 / Logistics

Import, warehouse, shipment

L-01

Import

Purchasing from vetted foreign suppliers, customs clearance in the name of DISPER LLC.

L-02

Pick-up or dispatch by a carrier to any region of Russia.
