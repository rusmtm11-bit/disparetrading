Auto parts importer and wholesale supplier

We bring goods from abroad, clear them through customs in our own name and supply businesses across Russia. A shorter chain means clearer prices and paperwork.

What we do

Dispare Trading (DISPER LLC, ООО «ДИСПЭР») imports auto parts and tyres and supplies them wholesale across Russia. Our customers are repair shops, service chains, retailers, distributors and companies with their own fleets.

Goods clear customs in our company's name, are stored at our warehouse in the Moscow region and are checked before shipment. Inside Russia there is nobody between customs and you but us, so we answer for every item.

  • Documents for every dealContract, invoice, UPD and closing documents.
  • Bank transfers onlyPayment to the company account with VAT, no cash.
  • Warranty in the contractDefect handling is agreed in advance.
  • E-documents and product markingDocuments go through an EDI operator; goods that require it are registered in Russia's "Chestny Znak" marking system.
