Personal data policy

This English translation is provided for convenience. The Russian version, adopted under Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data", prevails.

1. General provisions

This Policy sets out how personal data of individuals ("Data Subjects") that the personal data operator may receive through the website disparetrading.ru (the "Website"), including through feedback forms, is processed and protected.

The personal data operator is Limited Liability Company DISPER (ООО «ДИСПЭР»), INN 7733483947, KPP 773301001, OGRN 1267700136582, registered address: 71 Volokolamskoe Highway, bldg 4, premises 70K, Pokrovskoe-Streshnevo municipal district, Moscow 125424, Russia (the "Operator").

2. Data processed

When a request form on the Website is filled in, the Operator may process the following personal data:

  • name;
  • phone number;
  • email address;
  • company name (if provided);
  • any other data the Data Subject voluntarily includes in the message.

The Website does not request special categories of personal data (such as health or religious beliefs).

3. Purposes of processing

  • handling requests and enquiries received through forms on the Website;
  • preparing commercial offers and concluding supply contracts;
  • communicating with the Data Subject, including replying to enquiries;
  • performing obligations under concluded contracts.

4. Legal grounds

Personal data is processed on the basis of the Data Subject's consent, given by ticking the box in the form on the Website (Articles 6 and 9 of Law 152-FZ), and for the performance of a contract to which the Data Subject is a party.

5. Procedure and conditions

Personal data is processed with and without automation, including collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification, retrieval, use, transfer (provision, access), depersonalisation, blocking, deletion and destruction.

Personal data is processed and stored in the Russian Federation in accordance with Part 5 of Article 18 of Law 152-FZ. Personal data is not transferred to third parties, including cross-border, without the Data Subject's separate consent, except where expressly required by Russian law.

6. Retention period

Personal data is processed for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes listed in section 3 of this Policy and for the document retention periods established by Russian law (including accounting and tax law). The Data Subject may withdraw consent at any time.

7. Rights of the Data Subject

The Data Subject has the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of their personal data;
  • demand that personal data be clarified, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or not necessary for the stated purpose;
  • withdraw consent to processing;
  • appeal against the Operator's actions or inaction to the authorised body for the protection of data subjects' rights (Roskomnadzor) or in court.

8. Security measures

The Operator takes the necessary organisational and technical measures to protect personal data against unlawful or accidental access, destruction, modification, blocking, copying, dissemination and other unlawful actions of third parties.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The Website may use cookies and similar technologies to make forms work correctly and, if enabled, to collect anonymised visit statistics. Cookies are not used to identify a visitor unless the visitor voluntarily provides contact details in a form.

10. Consent to processing

By filling in a form on the Website and ticking the relevant box, the Data Subject confirms that they:

  • have read this Policy and agree to its terms;
  • consent to the processing of the personal data they have provided for the purposes listed in section 3;
  • have provided accurate data that relates to them personally.

11. Changes to the Policy

The Operator may amend this Policy. The current version is always available at disparetrading.ru/privacy (Russian) and disparetrading.ru/en/privacy (English translation).
