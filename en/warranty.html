Warranty and returns

Our goods come with a warranty. We handle defects and returns under the contract and the Russian Civil Code, without lengthy approvals.

Warranty

The warranty term and conditions are set out in the supply contract or in the documents for a specific batch. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and goods that do not match the stated specifications.

What counts as a defect

  • A manufacturing defect not caused by improper transport, storage or installation by the buyer.
  • Goods that do not match the part number or specifications in the order and accompanying documents.
  • An incomplete delivery through the supplier's fault.

Legal basis

The parties' relations are governed by the Civil Code of the Russian Federation, primarily its chapter on supply contracts, and in some cases by the Russian consumer protection law. Claim deadlines and the claim procedure are set in the contract with each partner.

How to return or replace

  1. Report the defectTo your manager or to distribution@disparetrading.ru: a description, the order number, photos or video.
  2. We review itWe look at the claim and, if needed, arrange an inspection or expert examination.
  3. ResolutionReplacement, refund or repair under the contract and the Civil Code.
  4. Return shippingAgreed case by case; costs are allocated by law and the contract.
