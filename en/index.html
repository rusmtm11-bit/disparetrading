How a supply works

  1. 1

    Request

    Send part numbers, a VIN or the make and model. If the item is in the store, you can order there straight away.

  2. 2

    Offer and contract

    We prepare an offer for your volume and sign a supply contract.

  3. 3

    Invoice and payment

    We issue a VAT invoice. Payment goes to the company bank account on the terms in the contract.

  4. 4

    Shipment

    Pick-up from the warehouse or a carrier to your region. The UPD transfer document arrives electronically.
