Terms of cooperation

Payment and delivery

We work with companies and sole proprietors under the standard B2B scheme: a contract, bank transfer and shipment by the method you choose.

01 / Payment
Payment methodBank transfer to the company account
TaxationVAT included; we issue VAT invoices and UPD (universal transfer documents)
Who can orderLegal entities and sole proprietors under contract
DocumentsSupply contract, invoice, closing documents after shipment
Document exchangeElectronic document exchange (EDI) with counterparties
Product markingVia the national "Chestny Znak" system for goods subject to mandatory marking

Payment terms (prepayment, deferred payment or partial prepayment) are fixed in the contract and can be agreed individually for regular partners.

02 / Delivery
Pick-upFrom the warehouse: Kuzyaevo village, bldg 2, Dmitrovsky district, Moscow region
By carrierA transport company of your choice, to any region of Russia
Lead timeDepends on stock availability and the chosen carrier
PackagingAccording to the carrier's requirements

Carrier delivery is paid separately at the carrier's rates unless agreed otherwise in the contract.

03 / Pick-up

Warehouse and pick-up point

Kuzyaevo village, bldg 2, Dmitrovsky district, Moscow region, Russia. Please agree the pick-up time with your manager before you come.
