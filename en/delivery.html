Payment and delivery

We work with companies and sole proprietors on the usual wholesale terms: a contract, payment against an invoice and shipment by the method you choose.

Payment

Payment terms (prepayment, deferred or partial prepayment) are set in the contract and can be agreed individually for regular partners.

How to payBank transfer to the company account
VATVAT included; we issue VAT invoices and UPD transfer documents
Who can orderCompanies and sole proprietors under contract
DocumentsSupply contract, invoice, closing documents after shipment
Document exchangeThrough an EDI operator
Product markingGoods subject to mandatory marking go through Russia's "Chestny Znak" system

Delivery

Carrier delivery is paid at the carrier's rates unless the contract says otherwise.

By carrierAny transport company you choose, to any region of Russia
